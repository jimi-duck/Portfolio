How I work on a product.

Based inBerlin, Germany
LanguagesEnglish · German (B2)

I studied product and industrial design, so I started with physical products, not screens. It taught me to watch the work before deciding what to build.

Each habit below comes from a product I worked on, and links to the case study that shows it.

  1. 01Discovery

    Watch the work before deciding what to build.

    At Enter I rode along on surveys and followed the data back through the office. At Coup there was no brief for the ops tools, so we started beside field workers.

    Enter →Coup Mobility →

  2. 02Problem framing

    Find the problem the brief missed.

    A forms tool at Enter, live within weeks, changed nothing: the accurate answer was still the slowest to give. Cooler Future’s research found two audiences where the brief had one.

    Enter →Cooler Future →

  3. 03Experiments

    Buy information before you buy engineering.

    A floor plan marked up in pencil answered in an afternoon what the real tool would have taken months to answer. At Coup, an in-app pilot let the business decide without building anything.

    Enter →Coup Mobility →

  4. 04Prioritisation

    Decide who wins before users disagree.

    Enter’s survey served the person on site, the homeowner and the energy model. Where they conflicted the model won, because an incomplete plan blocks the subsidy. Cooler Future’s sign-up kept only what the law required.

    Enter →Cooler Future →

  5. 05Working with engineering

    Agree the data before designing the screens.

    At Enter we settled what a survey produces with engineering and the AI team before designing any screen. At Vivy, a four-day sprint ended in requirements design, engineering and Allianz all agreed on.

    Enter →Vivy →

  6. 06Measurement

    Instrument the moment things go wrong.

    One in eight Coup rides was cancelled with no reason recorded; one tap on a list gave operations its first record. At Enter, an override of the model counted as a signal, not an error.

    Coup Mobility →Enter →
