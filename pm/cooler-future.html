The problem
Green investing was full of claims nobody could check. Cooler Future’s bet was trust: show exactly what each fund holds, and what it leaves out.
What I did
Led the design from day one. Research found two audiences the brief had missed, the regulated sign-up kept only what the law required, and impact claims stayed within what the data supported.
The result
A public launch on iOS, Android and web, open to anyone with €20, with every holding in the open.

01The business

Green investing had a credibility problem.

It’s easy to label a fund sustainable, and much harder to stand behind the label when someone looks closely. Cooler Future’s answer was transparency: what’s in each portfolio, what’s left out, and how it was checked, trade-offs included.

I joined before anything had been designed, so the first question was who we were building for.

02Research

The brief had one audience. There were two.

Interviews, diary studies and card sorts showed two audiences with opposite doubts. What each one knew, doubted and needed became the strategy, and it changed how the product spoke to people.

First-time investors

Curious but overwhelmed

“Is this actually better than leaving my money in the bank?” The product had to answer that itself, not on a separate education page.

Existing investors

Sceptical and evidence-driven

“How is this different from what I already have?” For them, transparency wasn’t decoration. It was the reason to trust us.

03Sign-up

Cut sign-up to what the law required.

A German depot account meant identity checks, tax details, KYC and disclosures, all mandatory. We postponed anything that could wait, cut what the law didn’t require, and told people what they’d need before each step.

After sign-up, people chose themes like Smart Energy before committing any money.

04Portfolio

One screen, both audiences.

The portfolio was where people came back most, so it had to work for both groups. It shows how the money is doing first, then the evidence behind it: sector allocation, exclusions, and how each fund was assessed.

05Impact

Show only what the data supports.

The link between a €500 investment and a real-world outcome is rarely direct. I explored goal summaries, supported-project feeds and carbon comparisons. The work stayed exploratory, but it set a rule the product kept: show only what the data supports.

Regulators, data providers and frameworks all measured impact differently, which made it hard to say anything both meaningful and defensible.

06Launch

The same trust, everywhere else.

The product launched on iOS, Android and web. Learn gave both audiences self-paced courses, and the website, usually the first thing anyone saw, carried the same transparency as the app.
